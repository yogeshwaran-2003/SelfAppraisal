Employee Self Appraisal – Rating Instructions

Employees should evaluate their performance based on measurable achievements during the appraisal cycle. The following goal categories define how ratings from 1 to 5 should be assigned.

1. Project Delivery Goals

Example: Develop and Deliver Project Modules

Score Condition
5 Completed 5 or more major modules/tasks before deadline with excellent quality
4 Completed 4 major modules/tasks on time with minimal corrections
3 Completed 3 modules/tasks on time with acceptable quality
2 Completed 1–2 modules/tasks but required multiple corrections or delays
1 Tasks not completed or incomplete work delivered
Evidence examples:
  • Project delivery reports
  • Git commits
  • Module completion screenshots
2. Code Quality / Bug Reduction Goals

Example: Reduce Bugs and Improve Code Quality

Score Condition
5 Fixed 20+ bugs and improved system stability
4 Fixed 15–19 bugs
3 Fixed 10–14 bugs
2 Fixed 5–9 bugs
1 Fixed less than 5 bugs or introduced new issues
Evidence examples:
  • Bug tracking reports
  • Issue tracker screenshots
3. Performance Optimization Goals

Example: Improve System Performance

Score Condition
5 Implemented 4 or more performance improvements (query optimization, caching etc.)
4 Implemented 3 improvements
3 Implemented 2 improvements
2 Implemented 1 improvement
1 No performance improvement implemented
Evidence examples:
  • Performance reports
  • Optimization documentation
4. Technical Skill Development Goals

Example: Complete Technical Certifications

Score Condition
5 Completed 3 or more certifications/training programs
4 Completed 2 certifications
3 Completed 1 certification
2 Attended technical training but did not complete certification
1 No learning or training completed
Evidence examples:
  • Certification certificates
  • Training completion reports
5. Knowledge Sharing / Leadership Goals

Example: Conduct Knowledge Sharing Sessions

Score Condition
5 Conducted 5 or more knowledge sharing sessions or mentored team members
4 Conducted 3–4 sessions
3 Conducted 1–2 sessions
2 Participated in sessions but did not lead any session
1 No knowledge sharing activities
Evidence examples:
  • Presentation slides
  • Seminar reports
6. Productivity / Task Completion Goals

Example: Complete Assigned Tasks Efficiently

Score Condition
5 Completed 15 or more assigned tasks within deadlines
4 Completed 12–14 tasks within deadlines
3 Completed 8–11 tasks within deadlines
2 Completed 4–7 tasks with delays
1 Completed less than 4 tasks
Evidence examples:
  • Task tracker reports
  • Project management tools
7. Attendance & Punctuality Goals

Example: Maintain Work Discipline

Score Condition
5 No late arrivals or absences during the appraisal cycle
4 Late 1–2 times
3 Late 3–4 times
2 Late 5–6 times
1 Late more than 6 times or frequent absence
Evidence examples:
  • Attendance records
8. Client / Stakeholder Satisfaction Goals

Example: Maintain Client Satisfaction

Score Condition
5 Received 3 or more client appreciation messages or positive feedback
4 Received 2 positive feedback messages
3 Received 1 positive feedback message
2 No feedback received
1 Received client complaint or negative feedback
Evidence examples:
  • Client emails
  • Feedback reports
9. Documentation & Process Compliance Goals

Example: Maintain Project Documentation

Score Condition
5 Prepared 5 or more detailed documents (design docs, manuals etc.)
4 Prepared 4 documents
3 Prepared 3 documents
2 Prepared 1–2 documents
1 No documentation maintained
Evidence examples:
  • Technical documentation
  • System design reports
10. Innovation / Process Improvement Goals

Example: Implement Process Improvements

Score Condition
5 Implemented 3 or more automation or improvement solutions
4 Implemented 2 improvements
3 Implemented 1 improvement
2 Suggested improvement ideas but not implemented
1 No innovation or improvement activity
Evidence examples:
  • Automation scripts
  • Improvement proposals
Important: Employees should assign ratings based on measurable achievements. If a rating of 4 or 5 is selected, supporting evidence or documentation must be provided. Managers will review the submitted information and finalize the rating.
